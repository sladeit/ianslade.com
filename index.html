About

My journey began as a clinician at the bedside, where I learned that the latest technology works best in caring hands. For more than twenty years I’ve worked to put it there, using technology to bring a better quality of life within reach for everyone. That work has taken many forms: technology consultant, global speaker, author, public policy advocate. The driver has always been the same: technology for the well-being of humanity. Storytelling is my favorite way to share what I’ve learned along the way, from the TEDx stage to my books to my seat on the board of the Hollywood Film Festival.

Policy and AI ethics

Useful and safe

I’ve helped shape health technology policy across three U.S. administrations: the HITECH Act under President Obama, the 21st Century Cures Act interoperability and information-blocking rules under President Trump, as chair of the HIMSS Advocacy Task Force, and the Partnership for Digital Access in Africa under the Biden administration. In California, as HIMSS SoCal’s VP of Advocacy, I helped brief legislators on AB 1979 and SB 503, two health AI bills signed into law in 2026. The common goal is technology that is useful and safe.

HIMSS named me its 2022 Global Advocate of the Year, and our Southern California chapter received the HIMSS National Advocacy and Public Policy Changemaker Award in 2025 and 2026. Today I carry that work into AI ethics, on the Vatican’s Rome Call for AI Ethics with Kathleen Kennedy Townsend and Father Paolo Benanti, the Pope’s AI advisor. I also teach at UCLA and speak on human-centered AI, from the TEDx stage to the Balboa Island Museum in Newport Beach.
